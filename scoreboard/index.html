How to read this

  • Since video compares the last closing price on or before the day the video went live with the latest close. Dividends aren't included.
  • Via means the shares belong to the company behind the brand, so the price reflects that whole business, not the brand alone.
  • Since listing marks a company that began trading after its video. It is measured from its first day's close and left out of the totals above.
  • Each price is in the stock's own currency, from Yahoo Finance, and may be delayed or occasionally wrong.

This is a record of what happened, not a prediction, a recommendation or investment advice. A price move doesn't prove a video right or wrong.
