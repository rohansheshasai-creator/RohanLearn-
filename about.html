My story

I started the channel because I kept explaining things anyway

For as long as I can remember, the way I learn something is by explaining it to someone else. Friends, classmates, anyone who'd sit still long enough. At some point it seemed sensible to just point a camera at it.

RohanLearn started as a way to hold myself accountable — if I said I'd make a video about something, I actually had to understand it properly first. It turned out that the videos other people found most useful were the ones where I hadn't figured it out yet, and you could watch me get there.

So that's what the channel is now. Not a expert handing down answers, but someone a few steps ahead on the same path, turning around and saying "here's the bit that confused me, and here's what finally made it click."

The best teacher isn't the one who knows the most — it's the one who still remembers what it felt like not to know.

How I work

Three rules I don't break

01

Say when I don't know

Confident guessing is how bad information spreads. If I'm unsure, that goes in the video.

02

Show the working

The mistakes and dead ends are usually the most useful part. They stay in.

03

Respect your time

No ten-minute padding for the algorithm. A video is as long as it needs to be.

The journey

How it's gone so far

  1. Before

  3. Since

    Finding the actual format

  4. Now
