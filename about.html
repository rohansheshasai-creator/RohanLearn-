My story

Built by a 17-year-old obsessed with how the world works

I've always wanted to know what's actually going on behind the headlines — why a company like BYJU'S can collapse under a $22 billion bet, why UPI is quietly beating Visa and Mastercard in India, why some brands go global and others fail the second they leave home.

A lot of that knowledge sits behind MBA programs and advanced studies, written in language that keeps most people out. RohanLearn is my attempt to take it apart and put it back together in the simplest way possible — so anyone can use it, not just people with a business degree.

Every video is built around real companies, real numbers, and 3 takeaways that can genuinely change how you see money, power, and strategy. No boring theory. Just what's actually true, explained simply — every Saturday.

No boring theory. Only real-world strategy.
