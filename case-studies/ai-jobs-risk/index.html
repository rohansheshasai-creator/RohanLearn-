Your Job Is at Risk Now: AI, Layoffs and Young Workers

For fifteen years, learning to code was the safest career advice on the planet. Now companies that aren't even losing money are cutting staff, and young workers are hit first.

The story

This breakdown looks at who is actually being cut, with real numbers: Stanford research on employment for 22-to-25-year-olds in AI-exposed jobs, tech layoffs running at around 981 a day, and a first decline in IT employment in two decades.

It also asks whether AI is really the reason, including evidence that people using AI tools can be slower while feeling faster, and why the World Economic Forum still expects millions of new jobs. The question isn't whether work disappears, but how its shape changes.
