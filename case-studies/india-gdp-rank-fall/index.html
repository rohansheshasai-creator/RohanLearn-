India's GDP Rank Fell. But Is This Planned?

India became the world's fourth-largest economy in 2023, then dropped to sixth in 2026. No war, no scam, no recession: it grew 8.2% and still fell two places.

The story

The video explains the one calculation behind the drop: GDP rankings are measured in US dollars, so a weaker rupee shrinks India's number even while its economy grows. It shows how American interest rates pushed the rupee down, and why a shrinking Germany can still rank above a growing India.

It then looks at India's rank by purchasing power, which tells a very different story, and ends with three practical things viewers can do to protect their own money.
