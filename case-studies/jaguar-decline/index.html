The Death of Jaguar: How a $2.3B Icon Failed

In January 2026 Jaguar registered just six new cars in the UK. A year earlier it was 783. This is how India's proudest turnaround nearly came undone.

The story

Tata Motors bought Jaguar Land Rover from Ford for $2.3 billion in 2008, when everyone called it reckless, and turned it into the group's biggest profit engine. The breakdown covers how big Jaguar still was, then the “Copy Nothing” rebrand launched with no cars on sale, and the 2025 cyberattack that stopped production for five weeks.

It then lays out three strategies Jaguar could use to recover, from leaving the BMW and Mercedes fight to treating operational resilience as part of the product.
