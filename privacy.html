Privacy

Your data, plainly.

This site has no accounts, no forms and no ads. Here is exactly what it measures, what it keeps on your device, and how to switch it off.

Visit analytics

To learn which parts of the site are useful, rohanlearn.com uses Microsoft Clarity. It records how visitors use the pages: which pages are opened, clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, plus general details such as device type, browser, approximate country and the site you came from. These recordings are anonymous: they are not linked to your name or email, and anything typed into a text box is hidden before it leaves your browser.

The site also notes a few anonymous moments, such as a Subscribe button being clicked, a “3 takeaways” box being opened or a calendar reminder being added, to see which features help.

Clarity stores two small first-party cookies (_clck and _clsk) to tell one visit from another. Microsoft processes this data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Analytics are on for this device. Switching off stops Clarity from loading on this browser.

Saved on your device

The site remembers a few choices in your own browser. They never leave it.

  • Light or dark mode.
  • Whether you closed the announcement bar or the subscribe bar.
  • Whether you switched analytics off.

YouTube

Videos play through YouTube's own player and open on YouTube. When a video plays, YouTube may set its own cookies under the Google Privacy Policy.
