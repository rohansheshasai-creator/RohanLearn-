What RohanLearn is really about

No fluff, no filler, no fake expertise. Just me figuring things out properly — and showing my work so you can skip the part where I got it wrong.

Explained from zero

Every video starts at the beginning. No assumed background, no jargon dropped without a definition — you'll never feel lost halfway through.

Actually researched

I read the thing, build the thing, or try the thing before I talk about it. If I'm unsure, I say so out loud instead of guessing confidently.
